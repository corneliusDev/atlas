# Sourdough Troubleshooting

Fixes for the problems I keep hitting with the loaf in [[Sourdough Bread]].

## Flat loaves
Usually an underactive starter. Feed twice a day for three days before baking, as in
[[Sourdough Starter Care]], and only use it at its peak (doubled, domed, smells sweet-sour).

## Dense, gummy crumb
Under-fermented dough. Extend the bulk ferment until the dough grows about 50% and
jiggles. Track temperature and timing in the [[Fermentation Log]].

## Too sour
Shorten the cold retard to 12 hours and keep the starter at a higher hydration. #sourdough #baking
