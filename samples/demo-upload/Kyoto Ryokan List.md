# Kyoto Ryokan List

Shortlist of traditional inns for the Kyoto leg of the trip, two nights between the
[[Tokyo Itinerary]] and the temple days in [[Kyoto Temples]].

## Shortlist
- **Gion area ryokan** with kaiseki dinner included; walkable to Yasaka Shrine at night.
- **Arashiyama riverside inn** with a private onsen bath; close to the bamboo grove for an early start.
- **Budget machiya stay** in Higashiyama; self check-in, no meals, fits the [[Travel Budget 2026]].

## Notes
- Book 60+ days out for cherry blossom season; cancellation windows are strict.
- The [[Japan Rail Pass]] covers the Shinkansen from Tokyo; the last stretch is a local train.
- Pack light: ryokan rooms have little storage, see [[Packing List]]. #japan #lodging
