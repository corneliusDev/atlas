# Pricing Experiment Results Q4

Results from the three-week test of the new tier layout described in [[Tier Design]]
against the current page.

## What we tested
- Variant B moved the usage-based add-on onto the Team tier (see [[Usage-Based Add-On]]).
- Annual discount shown as "2 months free" instead of 17% off, per [[Discounting Policy]].

## Results
- Trial-to-paid conversion up 11% on variant B; no change in refund rate.
- Expansion revenue from the add-on rose, which supports the seat-plus-usage [[Value Metric]].
- Churn in the first 60 days is flat so far; revisit against [[Churn Analysis 2026]] next quarter.

## Decision
Ship variant B to all new signups and update [[Pricing Page Copy]]. #pricing #experiments
